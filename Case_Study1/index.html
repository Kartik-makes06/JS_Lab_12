About SIH

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is a nationwide initiative that brings students together to solve real problems given by ministries, departments and industries.

It is organised by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell with AICTE.
Teams build either a software solution or a hardware prototype.

Idea → Team → Prototype → Pitch → Impact

Software Edition

Apps, portals and AI tools built in a 36-hour sprint.

Hardware Edition

Working prototypes and devices, with mentor support.

Junior Edition

Early innovators from schools get a platform too.

Challenges

Problem statements come from many fields. A few popular themes:

  1. Smart Agriculture and Food Technology
  2. Healthcare and Bio-medical Devices
  3. Clean Energy and Sustainability
  4. Smart Education and Skilling
  5. Disaster Management and Transportation

Tip: choose a statement you actually care about, it shows in the pitch.

Placements & Career

A strong SIH project boosts your resume. Recruiters value:

  • Teamwork under deadline pressure
  • Working prototypes and GitHub profiles
  • Presentation and pitching skills

Many finalists receive internships and job offers from startups and large companies.
